Technology and product updates
 
• Our flagship simulation software Eigenspace Design (ESD) continues to evolve rapidly, now approaching Beta stage, scheduled for academic edition release in January 2026.
• The software now integrates multiple physics modules and advanced SciML-based optimization tools, strengthening our foundation for next-generation multiphysics research.
• Our high-performance simulation engine now solves 100M+ degrees of freedom in under an hour, scaling efficiently to billions.
• Enhanced finite & isogeometric analysis tools enable seamless CAD-to-simulation integration for high-fidelity engineering workflows.• New topology optimization and cloud-native SciML modules offer faster design exploration, predictive modeling, and hyper-parametric tuning.
